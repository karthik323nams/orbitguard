# OrbitGuard Phase 8 — Earth-fixed visualization

Adds a GMST-based TEME → ECEF/PEF visualization transform, geodetic latitude/longitude/altitude, an Earth-fixed trajectory endpoint, and Earth-fixed coordinates in conjunction visualization.

## Scientific caveat
This is visualization-grade only: polar motion and higher-order frame corrections are ignored. It must not be presented as precision orbit determination or an operational conjunction product.
