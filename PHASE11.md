# OrbitGuard Phase 11

## Multi-object visualization
The dashboard can now render up to six Earth-fixed propagated objects simultaneously through `/visualization/multi`. When a conjunction is selected, the view switches to the existing two-object TCA scene.

## Historical analytics
`/conjunctions/history/analytics` aggregates stored scan runs and conjunction observations into:
- risk-band distribution
- mean prototype risk score
- minimum observed miss distance
- run/object/observation totals
- worsening/stable/improving/new trend counts
- top recurring object pairs

## Scientific framing
The multi-object view is a visualization/SSA prototype. Earth-fixed coordinates remain the documented GMST-rotated ECEF/PEF approximation. Historical risk analytics describe the project's explainable prioritization index and are not operational probability-of-collision assessments.
